🏹 Mind Arrows: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to Mind Arrows ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our puzzle game operates and clarifies our strict zero-personal-data retention model.

1. Information We Collect

Mind Arrows does not collect, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Your level completions, stars, coins, high scores, equipped skins, and locally entered nickname/age are stored strictly on your local device SQLite/Room database. None of this data is uploaded to any external server.
  • Device & Crash Diagnostics: Standard non-identifying telemetry (such as device model, OS version, or crash stack traces) may be processed anonymously by Google Play Services to improve stability.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mind Arrows displays ads via Google AdMob (including banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads). AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or IDFA for frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • Coarse network information and performance diagnostics.
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, reward validations).

To learn more about how Google AdMob processes information, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Mind Arrows is designed for puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has inadvertently provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

4. Data Security & Storage

All user progress, unlocks, and coins are retained within the Android sandbox environment. If you clear the app cache/storage or uninstall the application, your local game progress will be reset.

5. In-App Purchases (IAP)

If you purchase virtual items or ad-removal passes, transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never store or have access to your payment card details or financial information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
